About Peakfinity

Built on aggregation, not just ambition.

Peakfinity Enterprise exists to close the gap between Ghana's smallholder and cooperative producers and the international buyers who need consistent, graded volume. We don't grow every tonne ourselves — we build the aggregation, quality control and logistics discipline that turns scattered supply into a dependable export product.

Our starting commodities — shea butter, soybeans and cashew nuts — were chosen because each has strong existing production in Ghana, a clear global buyer base, and room for a sourcing partner who takes traceability and grading seriously.

Aggregation over ownership

We work with cooperatives and processing groups across producing regions, consolidating volume that no single farm gate could offer alone.

Grading before shipment

Every lot is checked against export specification — moisture content, purity, and free fatty acid or aflatoxin thresholds — before it is booked for shipment.

Fair, transparent buyback

Producer groups are paid on published, transparent price schedules, so the incentive to supply quality volume compounds over each season.

Built for repeat buyers

We are structuring Peakfinity around long-term offtake relationships, not one-off spot sales — consistency is the product as much as the commodity is.

Our Sourcing Network

Aggregation points across Ghana's producing belts

Rather than a single farm, Peakfinity's model is a network of cooperative aggregation points, each specialized in the commodity best suited to its region — then consolidated for export at central processing and inspection hubs.
